What it is

Playbook governs agentic workflows the way a franchise governs a team: objectives are declared with acceptance gates, work is played in bounded drives, and nothing counts as progress without independently validated evidence. The journal is the law — every decision is recorded, content-addressed, and replayable to an identical state.

The journal is the law: replay reproduces the state, and claims without evidence do not move the field.
