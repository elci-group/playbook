What it is

Playbook is the rulebook that governs agentic workflows, much like a franchise manual guides a team of operators. It defines the overall strategy, aligns incentives, and sets the framework within which autonomous agents operate.

In practice, Playbook declares clear objectives with built‑in acceptance gates, organizes work into bounded “drives,” and requires independently validated evidence before any step can advance. All actions are recorded in a content‑addressed, replayable journal, enabling transparent, auditable, and reproducible progress.
